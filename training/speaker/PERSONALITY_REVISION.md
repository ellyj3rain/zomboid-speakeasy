## What the current proof cannot establish

The expression proof's renderer does not use personality or strain to choose a
plan. Its 64 combinations preserve the same proposition order and cover every
part of the report. Version 4 wraps that witness into a task, and the data
preparer emits its text. That is useful admission plumbing, but no effect of
temperament, relationship or emotional state has been learned or measured.

The mandatory whole-report construction also cannot demonstrate selective
disclosure, goal-dependent emphasis, conversational repair or a change from
answering to asking, warning or refusing. Those require the broader learned
exchange already ratified in `SPEECH_ML_DESIGN.md`, not more variants of this
same renderer. The existing fact-binding controls remain regression checks.

## Concrete contrasts to develop

Each family starts with an evidenced person, knowledge, listener, situation and
task. Variations are declared authored interventions until backed by their own
captures. Related variants remain in one dataset split. The table defines
evaluation concerns, not hard-coded mappings from traits to phrases.

| Contrast family | What changes; what stays fixed | What a useful difference would show |
|---|---|---|
| Expression alternatives in the actual Mara scene | Same captured input and facts; compare authored phrasings | Whether fragmentation, hesitation or an expletive is plausible and useful, including that none may be needed |
| Same person across strain | Capture the existing pressure/energy state in ordinary company, work and threat; hold person and source knowledge stable | Different prioritization and economy while recognizable as the same person; existing register floors respected |
| Same person with another relationship | Evidenced listener relationship changes; source facts and immediate task stay stable | Appropriate disclosure and directness without automatic oversharing or invented shared history |
| Similar situation, different people | Real identity/history/effective traits differ; give comparable acquired facts | Distinctions beyond swapping fillers or punctuation; no identity labels serving as a shortcut |
| Goal and object relevance | Existing active purpose and perceived alternatives change; person and known report stay stable | A historical-report answer differs from deciding how to reach someone; missing current service evidence affects the decision |
| Adaptation over time | Same person before/after a recorded lesson, relationship event or changed evidence | Response to what was actually learned, preserving continuity; no automatic successful behavior |
| Interactions | Combine independently understood changes, such as trusted listener plus urgent work | Behavior is not the sum of isolated style switches; hold out combinations for evaluation |

For the first family, these are illustrative authored alternatives, unreviewed
and outside the current renderer's grammar. They are not new admitted tasks:

**Measured delivery:** “The July second paper said the phones were out across
Knox for hours. They were still down at press time. Businesses closed. No cause

**More fragmented delivery:** “The July second paper said the phones were out
across Knox. Hours, Jon. Still down at press time. Businesses closed. They hadn't
announced a cause. Wires, lightning, sabotage... I mean, that was talk.”

**Expletive as an optional reaction:** compare the measured answer with and
without “Damn.” at the end. Its presence alone is not evidence of anxiety,
aggression, realism or better communication. Compare the whole scene and effect.

## Evaluation and small-model preparation

Keep separate evidence for factual correctness, task relevance, continuity of
the person, adaptation to state/relationship, and quality of expression. Record
attention/claim selection, communication act and available-action choice with
their supporting evidence; do not mistake a plausible explanatory monologue for
an observed internal reasoning process or an executed action.

Word/sentence counts, fragments, fillers, repairs and expletives can describe
outputs. They are diagnostics, not quotas or a realism score. Response delay,
interruption and utterance timing require actual timing targets. Human review
judges the whole scene; an anxious person need not stutter or swear, and a
composed answer does not imply absence of anxiety.

Vary existing causes coherently before considering new variables. Compare
removing an input, changing it within supported states and combining it with
another influence. Measure whether each changes the relevant behavior without
changing factual entitlement. Identity-held-out and source-held-out evaluation
must not be replaced with random splits of paraphrases from one scene.

The ratified model arrangement remains one shared base with typed task adapters.
The operator's request for granular effects in small models is carried into
per-variable and interaction experiments. No independent model per trait is
assumed by this revision; changing model topology needs measured value, cost and
an explicit architecture decision. Frozen tokenizer/reference preparation can
advance alongside this work, but does not settle behavioral quality.

# Person-state comparisons

This first family connects existing game-owned state to four human judgments
about communication. The scenes and candidate replies are authored. The game
code supplies their captured state; no learned model produced the replies.
The exact review and its current standing are recorded alongside this document.

All four judgments have now returned. [The review and continuation](REVIEW.md)
preserve the exact notes and distinguish revision requests from qualified
preferences. The comparison below remains the original material that was judged;
its requested threat comparison is superseded by the broader priority-gradient
direction in the returned review. The original comparison stays training-ineligible.

Mara read a July 2 newspaper on July 10. On July 11, Jon asks: **“What did the
July 2 newspaper report about phone service?”** The report describes an outage
across Knox, businesses closing, service still down at press time and no
announced cause. Speculation about wires, lightning and sabotage stays
speculation. None of this establishes service conditions on July 11.

All five captures retain that report, person and question. The baseline carries
Mara's existing anxiety condition, effective temperament and neutral trust toward
Jon. A condition label does not quantify current anxiety or prescribe a voice.

Each card shows the literal alternatives and what the response would teach.
Both-fitting and neither-fitting responses are valid, with freeform amendments.
These are contextual preferences for authoring and evaluation. Answering does
not approve a speaker task, admit training data or establish a universal rule
linking trust, lessons or strain to phrasing.

## Source and model input

SAO C78's capture version 2 supplies detached channels with person, listener,
time, owner and availability. The source registry pins the published source
commit, capture and producer-manifest hashes; importing checks actual commit
blobs and installed game inputs. The comparison rebuilds its complete prepared
view from those verified imports. The original C77 import remains supported.

The model view includes effective temperament, conditions, relationship,
retained perceived threat, cognitive projections and the private claim
catalogue with readable report content. Incoming utterance roles retain Jon as
the questioner and Mara as its listener, distinct from capture ownership. Base/history/condition contribution
breakdowns and latent medical cause histories remain audit evidence. A told
lesson's existing private history claim carries its wording and provenance.
Changed channels are measured from the baseline rather than inferred from a
scenario's name.

These captures run production owners in installed Kahlua with controlled
bodyless people. Native needs, active work and movement goals are unavailable
in this family. SAO separately tests its loaded reader against controlled
boundary objects; that is not a loaded-world scene. Complete executable
alternatives are absent. The five variants share one source lineage and
cannot serve as independent training and evaluation partitions.

## Reproduction and continuation

`tools/behavior_comparisons.py` prepares `request.json` into `comparison.json`;
`--check` verifies exact reproduction. The comparison validates every source
import and remains `trainingEligible: false`. The checked-in Mousecat invocation
binds each judgment to the exact capture, producer manifest and literal
candidates used by this comparison, with the prior personality revision linked
in Mousecat history.
Private continuation capability is kept outside the repository.

The next expansion needs independent situations with available needs, current
work, goals and executable alternatives. The capture must follow the owners'
existing loaded/dormant contracts. Review feedback then informs supported
communication acts and task targets, followed by separate factual, task,
continuity and expression evaluation. Frozen tokenizer and reference preparation
can advance alongside that work under the ratified shared-base architecture.

# Voice material

Authored language of the county's people: how a guarded person and
an open one word the same fact, how trust changes an answer, how
grief shortens one - demonstrated in examples, expanded at build
time into training scale.

Rules:

- The register is plain and direct: a person talking. Character
  speech may carry character.
- The expansion tooling is deterministic and its output is
  reviewable; the seed is what gets reviewed and approved.
- The register follows the strain (SAO's DR-033, 2026-09-06): the
  seed must show the same person in ordinary company and at work
  or under threat - the second spending fewer words and weighing
  each; the floors that cut the register are SAO's and are
  bordered there.

Empty. Seeds follow the world documents.

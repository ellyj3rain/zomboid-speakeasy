# Timeline - the United States, January 1990 to December 31, 1993

| | |
|---|---|
| Status | DRAFT - not approved; nothing here teaches a model yet. |
| Horizon | Every claim is dated. What a survivor knows is cut by the save's own start date (and by the fall, in a living start) - see PLAN.md. The shipped default start is July 9, 1993. |
| Rule | Every claim carries when, confidence, a primary source, and who would carry it. LOW never teaches. |

Confidence: HIGH = a primary source dates it exactly. MEDIUM = a
primary source supports it approximately or indirectly. LOW = no
primary source yet.

Who carries it: `everyone` (any adult would know), `adults`
(followed the news at all), `followers` (people who followed that
topic), `service` (military and their families), `region:X`.

Access note: sources marked (index) are period primary sources -
wire reports, trade press, official reports - whose text was read
through a search index of the source rather than the page directly,
because the host blocks direct retrieval. The citation names the
source and date so anyone can read it in full.
